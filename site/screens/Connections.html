How the approved offer reaches CENACE

Pantheon offer → approval in Pantheon → [TRADER] (Market Participant) → CENACE portal, by 10:00 D-1 → cleared schedule back in the afternoon → CENACE dispatch instructions → plant EMS. Pantheon never controls the battery.

Selected: your market desk submits. The deadline shown everywhere is CENACE’s 10:00 cut-off.
