The rule the replay follows

1 · CENACE STATEMENT→2 · METER→3 · EMS→4 · MODEL

Replay uses, in order: CENACE statement → certified meter → EMS → model, and always says which. Money is only called reconciled from a statement. A replay on EMS data is labelled preliminary; one on the model is labelled estimate.

Known limits

  • EMS feed is the OEM portal's read-only export; a 5-minute lag is normal, over 15 minutes raises an alert.
  • Meter data arrives next morning; today's replay is preliminary until then.
  • Instruction log in the EMS may differ from what CENACE records; the ICCP mirror closes that gap.
