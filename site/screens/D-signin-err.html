SIGN IN · FOUR THINGS THAT GO WRONG

When sign-in does not work

Each state says what happened, whether the battery is affected, and who can fix it. None of them blames the person.

WRONG ACCOUNTSTATE 1
daniela@gmail.com is not a [CLIENT] account.

Pantheon only accepts company accounts. Sign in with your work email, or ask your admin which account was invited.

Contact Genau
SINGLE SIGN-ON FAILEDSTATE 2
[CLIENT]’s identity provider did not answer.

This is usually a company network issue, not Pantheon. Try again in a minute. If it keeps failing, your IT desk can check the SSO connection; Pantheon’s status page shows whether we see the same thing.

Contact Genau
ACCOUNT LOCKEDSTATE 3
Five wrong codes in a row.

Your account is locked for 15 minutes. Approvals for your sites continue with the other operators on shift. An admin can unlock it sooner.

Contact Genau
NO ACCESS YETSTATE 4
daniela@[client].mx is signed in but has no role on any site.

Someone with the admin role at [CLIENT] needs to invite you and pick your role. Mauricio Vega and [ADMIN] are admins.

Contact Genau

Rule: a sign-in problem never hides the deadline. If a plan is waiting, the state says so and names who else can approve it.
