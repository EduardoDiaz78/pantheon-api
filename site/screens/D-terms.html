Terms and privacy

Written to be read. The signed agreement prevails where the two differ.

1. What Pantheon does

Pantheon forecasts prices and solar output, proposes a day-ahead dispatch plan for your battery, and records what your people approve. It does not operate the battery and does not submit offers to CENACE unless a separate power of attorney says so.

2. Your approval, your decision

No plan is sent anywhere without a person at [CLIENT] approving it. Pantheon refuses plans outside the warranty terms you countersigned. Everything else is a proposal.

3. Data

Plant telemetry, meter data and statements remain [CLIENT]’s. Genau uses them to run the service and to score its own forecasts, keeps frozen forecasts and the audit log for 7 years, and shares nothing with third parties except an independent verifier, anonymised, if you allow it in settings.

4. Fees

Fees are a share of the reconciled gain over your schedule before Pantheon, as set out in Schedule A. No gain, no fee. Service credits apply as set out in the commitments table.

5. Liability

Genau is responsible for delivering plans on time, keeping within the limits you set, and keeping the record. Genau does not underwrite market prices, CENACE’s decisions, or plant availability. Liability is capped as set out in the agreement.

6. Privacy

Personal data (names, work emails, phone numbers, PIN hashes, sign-in records) is processed to run the service and secure it, under the Data Processing Addendum. People can see what Pantheon holds about them on their profile.
